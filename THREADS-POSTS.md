# Vonguul Clarity — Threads Tracker

Account: **@offbahr** on Threads (personal voice, not a brand persona — see note below).
Bio: "The only thing I'm dead to is the nonsense. No horoscopes. No fluff. Just mechanics."

Threads posting is now a standing step in the Vonguul Clarity content process,
alongside Pinterest. Unlike Pinterest (a disposable/anonymous-persona channel)
and Reddit (also a persona account), Threads runs through the real personal
account and voice of the site owner. Content must sound like a specific person
with opinions — first-person, casual, skeptical-but-engaged — not neutral
marketing copy. Match the existing tone in the account's bio and pinned posts
(mechanics over mysticism, direct, occasionally contrarian).

## Format

For each article, post a **mix** of two thread types, same as an organic
personal account would:

1. **Opinion thread** — a specific, first-person take related to the
   article's subject, tied to a real mechanism/fact from the article's
   "worth being direct about" section where one exists. **No link.** This is
   the majority of volume — it builds voice and following without reading as
   an ad.
2. **Article-linked thread** — ties a specific claim or product
   recommendation from the article to a direct link, tagged
   `?src=instagram` (already a supported channel in the site's tracking
   script, same convention as `?src=pinterest` / `?src=reddit`). Use these
   more sparingly than opinion threads — maybe 1 in 3.

Not every article needs both types on day one; the two can be spread out
across multiple posting sessions like the Pinterest batches are.

## How this fits the process for new articles/products

Whenever a new Vonguul Clarity article is drafted (new herb, crystal, practice,
product, etc.), the content process now includes, alongside the Pinterest
board/pin batch:

1. Add the new article as a row in this table (`not posted` / `not posted`).
2. Draft at least one opinion thread and one linked thread in the account's
   established first-person voice — always in an interactive session (same
   as Pinterest/Reddit posting), never fully automated, since this is a real
   personal account and voice, not a persona.
3. Post via threads.com, logged in as @offbahr.
4. Update this table and commit/push.

## Posting notes (from first batch, 2026-09-05)

- Threads' compose modal is sometimes slow to render after clicking
  "What's new?" — wait ~1s and re-screenshot before assuming the click
  failed; a second click on an already-open modal can misfire.
- Link previews take a couple seconds to resolve after typing/pasting a
  URL — wait for the preview card to render (image + title) before hitting
  Post, so a broken/blank preview isn't shipped.
- A newly posted thread can take a few minutes to consistently reflect in
  the profile's own feed view even after appearing to post successfully —
  don't assume a thread failed or was removed just because a same-session
  profile check doesn't show it right away. Confirm directly in the
  Threads app/site before re-posting, to avoid accidental duplicates.

## Writing notes

- Angle: archetypes are a vocabulary for noticing patterns in yourself and in stories, not a personality test
  or a fixed truth. Reading order: short introduction, then the autobiography or essays, then the primary sources.
- "Worth being direct about": Jung's archetypes and collective unconscious are interpretive ideas, not tested
  science, and they don't hold up as empirical claims. Useful for reflection, not diagnosis or therapy. If
  something is distressing, talk to a licensed professional. No health or efficacy claims.
- Be direct about the popular books: King, Warrior, Magician, Lover and the Myss cards take liberties and go beyond
  Jung. Check how each describes itself when building. Name Jung's own work on racial and cultural views only if
  the sources used for the article support the wording.
- Links: dream-interpretation-books, shadow-work-journaling-for-beginners, enneagram-for-beginners,
  tarot-for-beginners, oracle-cards-for-beginners.

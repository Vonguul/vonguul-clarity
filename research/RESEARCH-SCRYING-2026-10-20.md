## Recommended build

New Clarity article "Scrying for Beginners: Mirrors, Crystal Balls and What You're Actually Seeing":
Tyson's book, the black-mirror book, the crystal-ball book, the Crystalstile 3.94 in and 7.08 in
mirrors, the Amlong ball (described as reconstituted quartz) and the QFkris glass ball. Honest
section: what people "see" while scrying is the brain filling in patterns in low light (pareidolia,
the same thing that happens with the Ganzfeld effect); treat it as a focus and imagination exercise,
not a window onto anything. Note that glass and quartz balls work the same way for this.

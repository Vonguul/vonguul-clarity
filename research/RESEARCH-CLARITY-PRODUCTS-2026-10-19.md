# Clarity product research (2026-10-19)

Goal: more products for Vonguul Clarity, favoring higher-priced items where there is a real link
to a practice the site covers. Method: amazon.com search, then each product page read live
(rating, review count, stock, seller, category). None of these ASINs is used on either site yet.
Prices are internal only and must not appear in articles. Re-check stock at build time.

Commission notes (Amazon's published table, subject to the reported 2026 cuts): Books 4.5%,
Musical Instruments 3%, Sports & Outdoors 3%, Home 3%, Electronics "all other" 4%,
Health & Personal Care 1% (healing crystals are filed there).

## Recommended build order

1. New article: tarot decks after your first deck (7 decks + box).
2. Sound healing step-up section (crystal bowl set, Zildjian gong, Koshi chimes, large Tibetan bowl).
3. Home planetarium section (Homestar Flux, Homestar Classic) in the telescope or moon-phases guide.
4. Meditation seating section (chair, zafu/zabuton set, cushion, folding floor chair).
5. Optional: scrying article; amethyst statement pieces.

Each build: re-check stock, write with the site's honest framing, no prices, and owner approval
before push.

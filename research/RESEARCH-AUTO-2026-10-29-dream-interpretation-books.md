## Writing notes

- Structure: three ways to read a dream. (1) Symbol dictionaries (DeBord, Miller/Shields): fun and
  quick, but one fixed meaning per symbol. (2) Depth psychology (Freud for history, Jung and Johnson
  for a method): meaning comes from your own associations, not a lookup. (3) Sleep science (Zadra and
  Stickgold, Walker): what dreaming is in the brain.
- Worth being direct about: there's no evidence a dream symbol has a fixed, universal meaning, and
  dreams don't predict events. Zadra and Stickgold's NEXTUP model treats dreams as the brain exploring
  loose associations during sleep. Dream work is useful as reflection (what did this bring up for
  me?), not as decoding or prophecy. No health or therapy claims.
- Links: lucid-dreaming-getting-started (journals, recall), shadow-work-journaling-for-beginners
  (Jung), gift-ideas-tarot-astrology-human-design.

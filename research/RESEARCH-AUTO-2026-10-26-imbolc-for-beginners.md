## Writing notes

- Angle: Imbolc as a small, practical first festival after Yule: light a candle, clean one room,
  notice the first signs of spring where you live. Brigid as folklore figure and saint, with the
  books as the way in.
- Worth being direct about: much of modern Imbolc practice is a 20th-century reconstruction; what
  ancient Gaelic Imbolc involved is known only in fragments, and Brigid the goddess and Saint Brigid
  are tangled in the sources. Present rituals as reflection and seasonal rhythm, not as proven
  effects. No health or efficacy claims (Brigid is linked to healing in folklore: report it as

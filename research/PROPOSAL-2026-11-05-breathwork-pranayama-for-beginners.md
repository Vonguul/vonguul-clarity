## Angle

Breathwork is the most practical entry point into the contemplative practices on this site: it needs no belief and almost no gear.
The article explains the main beginner techniques (slow breathing, box breathing, alternate-nostril breathing from the yoga
tradition), what the traditions claim about prana and energy, and what has actually been studied. Then a short list: one or two
books, a timer or pacer, and the seat or cushion that makes sitting possible.

**Why now:** a spin-off of the meditation and yoga gear guides (the meditation guide already uses a mala as a breath counter), and
New Year intention-setting in January is when people look for a simple daily practice.

energy-body vocabulary), `sound-healing-singing-bowls-for-beginners`, `manifestation-law-of-attraction-for-beginners` (daily habit angle).

## Quality bar

- In stock, sold by Amazon or a reputable seller.
- Rating about 4.3 or better with a real review count.
- ASIN not already on either site (the meditation and yoga gear pages already carry cushions and a mala; ask for a duplicate check
  in a session).
- Books: prefer ones that separate the research from the tradition; note any listing that promises cures.

## Writing notes

- Free first: the techniques cost nothing, and the article should say so before any product.
- **Worth being direct about:** slow breathing has been studied for relaxation and short-term stress, with modest, mixed results.
  Claims that breathwork clears energy, moves prana, detoxes the body or treats any condition are not established. Present
  pranayama's energy language as the tradition's framework, used for reflection and attention, not as physiology.
- **Safety note (no health claims, just caution):** skip breath-holding and fast, forceful techniques if pregnant, with heart or
  lung conditions, or if dizzy; never practice them in water or while driving. Point readers to a doctor for anything medical.
- Keep it short: 4 to 6 product picks at most, since the honest answer is that very little gear is needed.

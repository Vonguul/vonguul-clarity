# Vonguul Clarity — Instagram Stories Tracker (VG-52)

Account: **@offbahr** on Instagram — same real personal account used for the
Clarity feed posts (`INSTAGRAM-POSTS.md`) and Threads (`THREADS-POSTS.md`).
Stories are a separate, additive channel: a traffic experiment (per the
Vonguul Operations Command Framework, Section 22.3) to send viewers straight
to a specific article via a link sticker, without touching the mission-first
`vonguul.com/support` bio link used by every feed caption.

Full posting process (Meta Business Suite, native-file-picker caveat, crop
choice, where the real Instagram link sticker actually lives) is documented
once in vonguul-picks' `INSTAGRAM-STORIES.md` — same process, same account,
just a different destination URL per article. Not duplicating it here.

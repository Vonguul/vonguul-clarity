**Title:** Indian or Japanese Incense? The Difference, and Why to Try Both

**Description:** Indian incense is a scented paste rolled on a bamboo stick: strong and sweet, like Nag Champa. Japanese incense has no stick in the middle, is thinner, usually gives off less smoke and has a quieter scent for a small room or a long session. Treat it as a ritual cue, ventilate the room, and never leave it burning unattended. #incense #meditationspace #ritual

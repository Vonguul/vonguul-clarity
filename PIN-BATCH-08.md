## Pin 2

**Title:** The White Sage Problem, and What to Actually Use Instead

**Description:** Wild white sage has faced serious overharvesting pressure, and smudging carries real spiritual significance in specific Indigenous traditions many practitioners have asked non-Native people not to appropriate. Rosemary serves the same smoke-cleansing purpose without either problem — an easy swap that costs nothing extra. #smokecleansing #rosemarysmudge #herbmagic #sustainableherbs

## Pin 3

**Title:** Craft Herbs vs. Tea Herbs Are Not the Same Thing (Read This First)

**Description:** A bag of dried botanicals sold for spell jars and candle making is not the same product as culinary-grade dried herbs sold for tea, even when it's technically the same plant. Craft-grade herbs aren't held to food-safety standards — buy actual food-grade herbs separately for anything you plan to ingest. #spelljars #herbmagic #witchcraftsupplies #magicalherbsafety
